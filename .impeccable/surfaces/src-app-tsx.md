# Surface brief: app shell (Library, Songs, Activity, panels)

Mode: Operate. Task: find a song fast, see what each playlist holds, track changes, browse.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The library is a festival bill. Playlists are billed by size (headliners, main stage, support), so the biggest ones lead and everything has a rank. It refuses the dark art-grid-with-green-accent music app. Signature move: searching re-bills the poster, reordering and lighting every playlist a song plays on.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-violet ground, tangerine as the action colour, electric violet for fields and selection, lime for added and hot pink for removed. Heads set in a condensed poster grotesk in caps, small humanist grotesk for working text. Flat colour fields, hairline rules, grain, a slow rotating ring motif. No glow halos, no gradient text, no glass.

STORY: The listener opens it at night with headphones on, sees the whole bill and what changed since last time, types a song, and the poster answers with where it lives and a one-tap jump into Spotify.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail (Library, Songs, Activity, account) on desktop, bottom bar on phone. Top: search and sync. Under it a ticker band of totals and latest changes. Then the Library title with live counts, collection chips, then Headliners as the three largest cards, followed by Main stage and Support in tighter grids.
